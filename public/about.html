Harpur Land Mapper

Version 5.6.1

Family land-mapping tool for visually comparing Harpur cadastral parcels with Google Satellite imagery and maintaining a private plot database.

VillageHarpur(199) CircleRajpur DistrictBuxar StateBihar SurveyCS — Cadastral Survey Sheets01 and 02

Version 5.6.1 features

  • Google Satellite base map.
  • CS Sheet selector for Sheet 01 and Sheet 02.
  • Dynamic BhuNaksha cadastral PNG refresh after pan or zoom.
  • Click a visible cadastral parcel to identify its plot number.
  • Selection pin appears at the clicked location and is labeled with the plot number.
  • Pin stays during pan/zoom and Save/Update.
  • Pin moves when another plot is selected.
  • Pin is removed after Delete or browser/app reload.
  • Save, update and delete local plot records.
  • Owner/family, local name and notes fields.
  • Google Maps center link saved for each plot.
  • GeoJSON parcel-boundary reconstruction from BhuNaksha WMS.
  • Responsive layout for phone, tablet and desktop browsers.

Important note

This application is for family reference, visual matching and record keeping. Reconstructed parcel geometry and satellite alignment should not be treated as an official legal survey or replacement for government land records.
